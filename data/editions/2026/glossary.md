**Basis trade —** a leveraged trade that profits from the small price gap between US Treasury bonds and Treasury futures, financed with very short-term borrowing.

**Repo —** the overnight market where institutions borrow cash against securities; the funding that keeps much of the leverage in this brief alive.

**Haircut —** the safety margin a lender demands on collateral. A zero haircut means no cushion if prices move against the borrower.

**MOVE index —** a gauge of expected US Treasury-market volatility — the bond market's equivalent of the VIX.

**Carry trade —** borrowing in a cheap, low-rate currency (often the yen) to invest in higher-yielding assets elsewhere; profitable until the funding currency moves.

**Private credit —** loans made by non-bank funds directly to companies, outside public markets and with less frequent valuation.

**Stablecoin —** a crypto token pegged to the dollar, typically backed by reserves that increasingly include US Treasuries.

**Clearinghouse (CCP) —** an institution that stands between buyers and sellers to guarantee trades; efficient in normal times, a concentrated single point of failure in a crisis.

**Maturity wall —** a cluster of debt all coming due in a short window, forcing refinancing at whatever rates then prevail.

**Mark-to-market —** valuing an asset at its current market price rather than its original cost; 'extend and pretend' delays this reckoning.

**Credit spread (OAS) —** the extra yield investors demand to hold risky debt over safe government debt; widening spreads signal rising default fear.

**Sovereign spread —** the gap between one government's borrowing cost and a benchmark (e.g., French OATs over German Bunds); a market gauge of fiscal risk.

**De-dollarization —** the gradual shift by central banks and governments away from the US dollar as a reserve and settlement currency, often toward gold.

**Chokepoint —** a narrow node — a strait, a mine, a single supplier — through which a large share of global supply must pass, giving whoever controls it leverage.

**Grid interconnection —** the queue and physical process for connecting new power demand or supply to the electricity grid; a binding bottleneck for AI data centers.
