The highest-signal indicators to watch, by mechanism. Most are public and free; the live tracker monitors many of them automatically.

### Funding & Leverage

- The MOVE index: sustained spikes in Treasury volatility are what trigger the margin-and-haircut doom loops in the basis and carry trades.

- Repo rates and the SOFR–fed funds spread at quarter-ends: early signs of dealer balance-sheet strain and tightening funding.

- SEC Treasury central-clearing milestones (2026–2027) and Bank of Japan policy with 30-year JGB yields: structural shifts in the leverage backdrop.

- Large stablecoin redemptions or a depeg: the crypto-native version of a funding run.

### Credit, Consumers & Valuations

- AI vendor-financing and special-purpose-vehicle disclosures: the circularity becomes visible when revenue assumptions slip.

- Commercial-real-estate refinancing volumes and regional-bank CRE exposure: where the maturity wall meets thin capital.

- Subprime-auto and buy-now-pay-later delinquencies: the consumer stress that does not show in headline credit data.

- Insurer non-renewals and withdrawals in exposed states: the quiet erosion of home insurability and value.

### Sovereigns & the Monetary Order

- Emerging-market sovereign spreads and new IMF programs: where developing-world debt distress surfaces first.

- The French OAT–Bund spread and budget votes: the eurozone's nearest fiscal-political fault line.

- Official gold purchases and reserve-currency shares: the pace of the hedge against the dollar.

- Pension funding ratios and forced contribution increases: the slow, large strain on public balance sheets.

### Geoeconomics & Trade Weapons

- Rare-earth and critical-mineral export curbs or quotas: availability is not access.

- ASML order flow and chip export-control announcements: the lithography chokepoint in motion.

- War-risk and shipping insurance rates through Hormuz, the Red Sea and the Taiwan Strait: the price of sea-lane risk.

- China's producer-price index and export prices: the gauge of exported deflation.

### Physical & Resource Limits

- Data-center power-queue and grid-interconnection backlogs: the binding limit on the AI build-out.

- Copper inventories and mine-supply guidance: the structural deficit behind electrification.

- Reservoir and aquifer levels in chip-fabrication and farming regions: the underpriced water constraint.

- Fertilizer feedstock (natural gas, ammonia) prices and grain-export concentration: the fragility in the food system.
